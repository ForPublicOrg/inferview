## Question

The search corpus is 100 million documents averaging 4,000 tokens each (scenario). It is being moved to text-embedding-3-small at 1,536 dimensions, written into a new namespace on Pinecone serverless so the old one keeps serving until cut-over. Each chunk carries permission metadata: the IDs of the groups that may read it, plus source, document version and path.

Choose a chunk size and the metadata bytes per chunk, and estimate the one-off cost of the re-index: embedding every chunk plus writing every record, in thousands of US dollars. Then say what the new namespace costs to store per month while both run.

Published inputs: $0.02 per million embedding tokens (OpenAI, checked 2026-10-28); 1 write unit per KB upserted and record size of ID + metadata + dimensions × 4 bytes (Pinecone docs); $4 to $4.50 per million write units and $0.33 per GB-month (Pinecone Standard, checked 2026-10-28).

## Answer

<Depth>
<Fragment slot="short">
<p>About $31,000 at 500-token chunks and 1,000 bytes of metadata: $8,000 to embed 400 billion tokens and about $23,000 to write 800 million records of 7.18 KB. Storage for the new namespace is about 5.7 TB, roughly $1,900 a month. The writes, not the embeddings, are the dominant term.</p>
</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="long">
<p><strong>Embedding is fixed by the corpus.</strong> 100 million × 4,000 = 400 billion tokens whatever the chunk size (ignoring overlap), and 400,000 × $0.02 = $8,000.</p>
<p><strong>Writes depend on both choices.</strong> At 500-token chunks there are 400 billion / 500 = 800 million records. A record is 36 bytes of ID + 1,000 bytes of metadata + 1,536 × 4 = 6,144 bytes of vector = 7,180 bytes, about 7.18 WU. 800 million × 7.18 = 5.74 billion WU, × $4 per million = $22,976. Total about $31,000.</p>
<p><strong>Storage while both run.</strong> 800 million × 7,180 bytes ≈ 5,744 GB, × $0.33 ≈ $1,900 a month, on top of the old namespace.</p>
<p><strong>The band.</strong> 1,000-token chunks with 200 bytes of metadata give 400 million records of 6.38 KB, about $10,200 of writes and $18,200 in total. 300-token chunks with 2,000 bytes of metadata (documents shared with dozens of groups) give 1.33 billion records of 8.18 KB, $43,600 to $49,100 of writes and $51,600 to $57,100 in total. Anything from about $15,000 to $60,000 follows from reasonable choices.</p>
<p><strong>Unreasonable choices.</strong> Chunks above 8,192 tokens exceed the model's input limit. Metadata listing user IDs instead of groups makes records tens of KB for widely shared documents, and Pinecone's filter caps $in at 10,000 values anyway. Leaving metadata out entirely means the new namespace cannot enforce permissions on day one.</p>
<p><strong>Dominant term and what flips it.</strong> Write units dominate: the vector alone is 6,144 bytes, so every record costs at least 6 WU, and halving chunk size doubles the write bill while the embedding bill stays at $8,000. Two assumptions flip the conclusion. Upserting into the existing namespace instead of a new one adds 1 WU per KB of each record overwritten, roughly doubling the writes. A self-hosted index has no per-write charge, and then the $8,000 of embedding and the engineering time are the whole bill. Shortening vectors with the dimensions parameter cuts the 6,144-byte term, if the recall eval allows it.</p>
</Fragment>
</Depth>

## Common mistakes

- Pricing only the embedding API and calling a model change cheap.
- Forgetting that permission metadata is stored and written with every chunk.
- Upserting in place without counting the overwrite charge.
- Choosing chunk size for cost without checking it against the retrieval eval.
