## Question

<Callout kind="interview" label="Interview question">
"We are copying Llama 3's mix for a 15T-token run. Turn the percentages into tokens and epochs per source, and tell me which source is the problem."
</Callout>

Published inputs: Llama 3 reports roughly 50% general, 25% maths and reasoning, 17% code and 8% multilingual, and a corpus of about 15T tokens (Llama 3 paper, checked 2026-10-28).

Scenario inputs, after dedup and filtering: general web 18T tokens, code 2.0T, multilingual 1.5T. The maths-and-reasoning pool depends on the classifier threshold you choose:

| threshold | 0.9 | 0.8 | 0.7 | 0.6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tokens kept | 0.9T | 1.25T | 1.6T | 2.4T |

A two-seed ablation found the 0.6 tier worse than 0.7 by more than the seed spread; the tiers from 0.7 to 0.9 were within it. Choose a threshold, then give the epochs the maths-and-reasoning pool is seen.

## Hints

<details class="hint"><summary>Hint 1</summary><p>Ask whether the mix is a hard target per bucket or a ceiling the trainer may undershoot.</p></details>
<details class="hint"><summary>Hint 2</summary><p>Ask what the ablation said about each tier, and how far apart the seeds were.</p></details>

## Answer

<Depth>
<Fragment slot="short">
<p>Maths and reasoning is 25% of 15T, 3.75T. At threshold 0.7 that is 3.75 / 1.6 = 2.3 epochs; at 0.9 it is 3.75 / 0.9 = 4.2. Every other source is at or near one epoch, so this bucket is the problem, and the threshold decides whether it sits inside or past the range where repetition was measured to be close to free.</p>
</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="long">
<p>Tokens per bucket: general 7.5T, maths and reasoning 3.75T, code 2.55T, multilingual 1.2T, which sums to 15T. Epochs: general 7.5 / 18 = 0.42, code 2.55 / 2.0 = 1.28, multilingual 1.2 / 1.5 = 0.8. The maths pool gives 4.2, 3.0 or 2.3 epochs at thresholds 0.9, 0.8 and 0.7.</p>
<p>The dominant term is the maths share times the budget against the maths pool: 3.75T demanded from a pool between 0.9T and 1.6T. Doubling the general pool would change nothing; a 10% change in the maths share moves the answer by 10%.</p>
<p>The assumption that flips the conclusion is the threshold. At 0.7 or 0.8 the bucket stays at or under 3 epochs and the mix can ship as written. At 0.9 it passes 4 epochs, outside the range Muennighoff et al. measured as close to free, and the answer becomes: lower the maths share, or add rephrased maths, before the run.</p>
</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="followups">
<ul>
<li>The maths team wants 0.9 because its samples read better. What do you trade, in tokens and epochs, and what would you need to see to agree?</li>
<li>Llama 3 annealed on high-quality maths at the end of training. Does that change how many epochs of the maths pool you accept in the main phase?</li>
</ul>
</Fragment>
</Depth>

**Reasonable choices.** Thresholds 0.7 to 0.9 give 2.3 to 4.2 epochs, and the band admits all three, because the ablation could not tell those tiers apart. Choosing 0.7 is the defensible default: it is the largest pool with no measured harm. Choosing 0.9 is defensible only with a plan for the epochs above 4.

**Unreasonable choices.** Threshold 0.6 gives 1.6 epochs and looks comfortable, but the ablation measured that tier as harmful; it trades a known quality loss for an unmeasured repetition risk. Counting part of the 18T general web as maths to dilute the epochs double-counts tokens across buckets. Answering with epochs over the whole corpus (15T over 23.1T of pools at threshold 0.7, 0.65) hides the one bucket that matters.

**Where this estimate fails.** It assumes the mix is enforced per bucket across the whole run. Llama 3 also anneals on high-quality maths and code at the end, which adds repetition of the best slice on top of the main-phase epochs, and Muennighoff's range was measured on loss up to 9B parameters, not on maths accuracy at 15T.

## Follow-ups

- *The run is extended to 18T with the same mix. Which bucket crosses 4 epochs first, at which threshold?*
- *Code is at 1.28 epochs and the code team asks for 22% instead of 17%. What happens to code epochs, and which bucket do you take the 5 points from?*
- *What would you need to see in a small run to accept 5 epochs on the maths pool?*

## Common mistakes

- Reporting tokens per bucket without dividing by the pool, so repetition is invisible.
- Averaging epochs over the corpus.
- Choosing the cleanest tier without computing the epochs it forces.
- Quoting the 4-epoch finding as a hard limit rather than the edge of what was measured.
