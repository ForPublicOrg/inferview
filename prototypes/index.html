Nine tracks in one order, skipping what you already know

Written for experienced software engineers. No calculus, no computer vision, no speech. Track 1 is skippable after a short placement check.

Why people come back

The reading material for AI engineering already exists and much of it is excellent. What is missing is practice that grades you, in an order, with a reason to return tomorrow. That is the whole product.

Predict before you see the answer
Every widget asks for your guess first. That is the moment an interview tests, rehearsed on every page.
Graded in your browser
Python runs in Pyodide against hidden tests. Agent and RAG debugging runs on recorded traces. No API key, no account.
Questions come back on a schedule
Each question you rate becomes a spaced-repetition card. The daily problem is seeded by the date, so everyone gets the same one.
Answers at three depths
The 30-second conclusion, the 3-minute version with trade-offs, then the follow-ups interviewers ask: how do you verify it, how does it fail, what is the alternative.
Company tags are free and dated
Each tag says whether it came from a candidate report or the author, and when. Elsewhere this is the paid tier.

Temperature is a knob on a histogram

The model does not pick a word. It outputs a probability for every token in the vocabulary, and the sampling settings decide how to draw from that table. Drag temperature and watch the distribution flatten or spike. Lower top-p and watch tokens drop out of the draw. This is the widget from the sampling lesson; it runs on fixed example logits.
